About the Hub & Developer

Built by Nimra (NimraDev)

Hi! I am Nimra, an independent web developer. I created NimraDev Hub (nimradev.site) to build clean, fast, and privacy-respecting digital tools for students, creators, and developers worldwide.

So many utility websites today are overloaded with intrusive ads, paid subscriptions, or require you to upload personal documents and student records to unknown remote servers. My goal with this hub is simple: build tools that run locally in your browser using modern web technologies (JavaScript, HTML5 Web APIs, and WebAssembly) so your data stays in your hands.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using NimraDev Hub.

Are the tools on NimraDev Hub free to use? ↓

Yes, 100%. All tools on NimraDev Hub and its connected platforms (PDFora, PU GPA Calculator, and in-browser utilities) are completely free with no hidden paywalls, subscriptions, or account requirements.

Are my documents or inputs uploaded to any server? ↓

No. The utilities operate client-side directly inside your browser. Your files, texts, and calculations are processed in your local computer RAM and are never sent or stored on any server.

How does the Punjab University (PU) GPA Calculator calculate grades? ↓

The calculator uses official Punjab University semester grading rules, converting course marks into standard Grade Points (GP) and weighing them by each course's credit hours to yield your exact semester GPA and cumulative CGPA.

Can I request a new tool or report a bug? ↓

Absolutely! If you have an idea for a useful web tool or found an issue, please reach out through our Contact Page. I love hearing suggestions from users.
