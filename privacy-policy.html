Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

At NimraDev (accessible at nimradev.site), we are deeply committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning data collection, client-side processing, and security across our digital tools ecosystem.

2. In-Browser Client-Side Processing

Our web applications operate using local browser sandboxing. When you utilize tools on our network, files and inputs are computed directly inside your device's memory. We do not transmit, retain, or store your private documents on remote servers.

3. Cookies & Analytics

We may utilize standard non-identifying cookies and aggregate analytical metrics strictly to monitor site availability, prevent malicious abuse, and optimize platform performance.

4. Third-Party Services & Ad Networks

We partner with third-party vendors such as Google AdSense to serve relevant advertisements. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on prior visits. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out directly via our Contact Page.
