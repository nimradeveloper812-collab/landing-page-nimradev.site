Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

Welcome to NimraDev Labs (accessible at nimradev.site). We develop high-performance, privacy-first web utilities and productivity suites, including our flagship platform PDFora (pdfora.nimradev.site). We are dedicated to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring complete transparency regarding how data is handled.

2. Client-Side Security & Zero Data Retention

All web utilities engineered under the NimraDev Labs ecosystem leverage modern browser sandboxing, WebAssembly (Wasm), and client-side execution APIs.

Your files, documents, text, or calculations are processed locally inside your web browser. We do not inspect, retain, harvest, or store your documents on remote servers. You retain 100% control and ownership over your data at all times.

3. Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

To keep our tools, educational resources, and platforms 100% free and accessible to users worldwide, we utilize Google AdSense to display advertisements.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to nimradev.site, connected subdomains, and/or other sites across the World Wide Web.
  • Ads displayed are intended to be non-intrusive and strictly comply with the Google Publisher Policies and the Coalition for Better Ads standards.

Opting Out of Personalized Advertising:

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting:

Google Ads Settings → AboutAds.info Choices → Network Advertising Initiative →

4. Analytics & Non-Personal Diagnostics

We may collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic metrics (such as browser version, operating system, page views, and performance timings) to diagnose system stability and maintain uptime. This information is purely aggregate and never tied to personal identity.

5. User Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

In compliance with international data privacy regulations including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • You have the right to request information regarding any personal correspondence sent to us.
  • You have the right to request deletion of support correspondence.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data to third parties.
  • European users are presented with standardized consent mechanisms (CMP) in adherence to Google's EU User Consent Policy.

6. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

NimraDev Labs does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. All utilities are educational and productivity-oriented tools suitable for general audiences.

7. Contact Information

For inquiries, feedback, or data privacy requests, contact us at: contact@nimradev.site or visit our Contact Page.
